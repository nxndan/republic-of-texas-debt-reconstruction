# Paper outline (8-12 pages, chronological)

Working title: **Who Took the Haircut? Scaling, Federal Settlement, and the Republic of Texas Debt, 1836-1856**

Write each section in your own words. Cite a source and page for every number. Quote at most a short phrase.

1. **Abstract (150 words).** Question, data, method, one-sentence finding, one-sentence limitation.
2. **Why Texas borrowed, 1836-1845.** Use purpose_of_debt_1836.csv and Miller's expenditure chapters (defense, navy, frontier and ranger costs, civil list). State what you checked on the page scans.
3. **The instruments.** One paragraph per instrument in debt_instruments.csv: what it was, rate, size, how it was treated.

5. **The Compromise of 1850 and the Act of 1855.** $10M in 5% federal bonds for the land cession; $5M held back pending creditor releases; the $7.75M cash appropriation; Texas voters' rejection and legislative acceptance.
6. **Analysis.** Table and chart from outputs/. Compare state-scaled value with the flat federal payout by instrument. State the uniform-proration assumption explicitly.
7. **Limitations.** Secondary-source numbers; the unitemized remainder (resolved or not); the proration assumption; no present-value adjustment (unless you add one); nothing here covers who ultimately held each instrument.
8. **Conclusion (one paragraph).**
9. **Bibliography.**
