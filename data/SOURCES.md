## What Miller (1916) says about the proration
Miller (1916, pp. 63, 64, 74, 79) describes the revenue debt as paid pro rata from the $7,750,000 reserved in the U.S. Treasury, at approximately 76.9 cents on the dollar, under the Act of February 28, 1855 (U.S. Statutes at Large, vol. X, ch. 129, p. 617) and Texas's assenting act of February 1, 1856. This supports the uniform-rate modeling assumption as secondary-source evidence. The original statutory text has not yet been independently examined, so the calculated instrument-level federal allocations should not be presented as independently verified historical disbursements.
