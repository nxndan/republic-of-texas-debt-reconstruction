## What Miller (1916) says about the proration
Pp. 63, 64, 74 and 79 say the revenue debt was paid pro rata out of the $7,750,000 reserved in the U.S. Treasury, at 76 9/10 cents on the dollar, under the act of February 28, 1855 (U.S. Statutes at Large, vol. X, ch. 129, p. 617) and Texas's assenting act of February 1, 1856. This supports the flat-rate assumption but is secondary; the Act itself is still to be read.
